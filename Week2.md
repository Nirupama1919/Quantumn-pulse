Project 2 – Enterprise Financial Dashboard

1. HCI Research & Architecture – Progressive Disclosure

## Introduction

Progressive Disclosure is a UX design technique that displays important information first and provides additional details when users need them.

# Research Activities

- Studied the concept of Progressive Disclosure.
- Identified three levels of information visibility.
- Researched how to organize financial information.
- Studied ways to reduce dashboard complexity.
- Identified suitable interactions for financial data.

Three Levels of Progressive Disclosure

1. Visible by Default: Total Revenue, Total Expenses, Net Profit and financial alerts.
2. Hover or Tap: Additional chart details, percentage changes and financial summaries.
3. Click: Detailed transaction history, financial reports and complete records.

## UX Design Rules

- Display important information first.
- Keep the interface simple and organized.
- Use clear labels and meaningful icons.
- Provide additional details through interactions.
- Avoid hiding important financial alerts.
